Full-width coloured bar opening a unit section page, with a tilted white title tag at its left end.

```jsx
<UnitTitleBar number={5} color="var(--color-teal-5)">¡Fiesta!</UnitTitleBar>
```

Variants: bar colour follows the section accent: teal-5 (ConTextos), orange (Gramática), others as the section tab.
