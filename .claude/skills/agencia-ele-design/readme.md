# Agencia ELE design system

A design system for a Spanish-as-a-foreign-language coursebook page, inferred from `agencia4.pdf` (24 pages, Adobe InDesign CS5, Spanish, portrait 591.9 x 804.5 pt). Medium: **publication-print**. Every token and component is tagged **observed** (measured from the PDF, with page references), **estimated** (derived or sampled) or **inferred** (judgement, with confidence), in `provenance.json` and below.

## Sources

- `agencia4.pdf`, 24 pages, vector text layer (strategy `vector-exact`), 54 embedded photos and illustrations (never shipped), 23 font families (50 subset-embedded, never shipped).
- Approved analysis: `_analysis/analysis.md` (148 items kept, 0 dropped, inferred items kept). Machine record: `_analysis/approved.json`. Page renders and crops live in `_analysis/` (git-ignored).
- Evidence labels in `provenance.json`: `p.N` are PDF page numbers (1 = cover), `n=` is the occurrence count.

## Intentional additions

Inferred items, all from the approved analysis. Aliases live in `tokens/roles.css`; descriptive findings are carried by guideline cards.

- **U-001** type scale (confidence high): `--type-scale-body/instruction/small` alias observed sizes; `--type-scale-caption: 8pt` is fitted. Card: `guidelines/type-scale.card.html`.
- **U-002** example text role (high): `--role-example-text` = example-blue, italic.
- **U-003** grammar accent (medium): `--color-role-grammar-accent` = orange (generated by scaffold in `colors.css`).
- **U-004** section accents (medium): `--section-accent-*` map the seven tab colours to the nearest observed token. Card: `colors-section-accents.card.html`.
- **U-005** 13pt baseline (medium): `--space-spacing-baseline`.
- **U-006** bullet semantics (low): lime circle = example, orange square = reply option; only seen on p.8, treat as a hint. Aliases `--bullet-example`, `--bullet-reply`.
- **U-007** note card style (medium): `--note-card-tilt` -3deg, `--note-card-radius` 3pt, `--shadow-soft` built from the observed 0.35 opacity (the real shadow is not measurable).
- **U-008** photo treatment (medium): `--photo-frame-width` 3pt, `--photo-frame-radius` 5pt. Photos are not shipped.

## Content fundamentals

Document language: **Spanish (es)**, one language. Quoted from the PDF:

- **Voice and person.** Instructions are imperative and address the learner informally: "Lee y relaciona cada imagen con la fiesta que describe." (p.8), "Escucha a estas tres personas…" (p.19); group work uses the plural: "Relacionad cada tipo de enlace con su definición.", "Tenéis que decidir el tipo de fiesta…". Questions use the inverted mark: "¿Qué tipo de fiesta organizarías?".
- **Casing.** Sentence case everywhere, including titles: "¡Qué fiesta!", "Cuento contigo", "Al final de la unidad…". Unit labels and the vertical unit name are the only capitals ("UNIDAD 5"). Exclamations carry the inverted mark: "¡Sorpresa!".
- **Numbering.** Exercises: bold number (1, 2); parts: bold letters a, b, c; sub-questions: bold numbers 1, 2, 3 under a part. Page numbers appear twice: "48 | cuarenta y ocho".
- **Bullets and emphasis.** Round bullets for lists, "•" between phrases in word boxes, a trailing ellipsis character "…" for open endings ("Yo estuve en una fiesta…"). Example and model sentences are italic blue; the form under study is bold ("va a ser", "podré").
- **Register of examples.** Colloquial, first person, conversational ("Pues a mí me gustaría una fiesta solo con mis mejores amigos"), set against a neutral instruction voice.

## Visual foundations

- **Palette logic.** Ink `#231F20` on white paper carries nearly all text. Colour is functional: one accent per section (tab, headings, table header), pale tints (`teal-tint`, `green-tint`, `red-tint`, `yellow-3`) behind boxes and notes, steel blue `#2F5C93` for folio and structure, example blue `#0072BC` for the learner's language. 40 observed colours are tokens; the rest of the long tail was dropped to the "not listed" tail (see analysis).
- **Backgrounds.** Plain white pages for exercises; full-bleed pale blue-grey (`teal-3`) panels with white circles on the introductory pages (p.2-5). Images are photographs and cartoons, cut out or framed.
- **Typography.** Humanist sans (Officina Sans) at 10.5pt on a 13pt line for body, 11.5pt for instructions, 12pt bold in the accent colour for exercise headings, 10pt for small text, 8pt for unit labels; handwriting faces for notes. Substitutes only, see below.
- **Spacing.** 13pt baseline; page 591.9 x 804.5 pt with margins about 47pt left, 38pt right (estimated); content in one or two columns; side tab on the outer edge.
- **Corners and borders.** Mostly square with 0.5pt hairlines; 3-5pt for note cards and photos, 12pt for word boxes, full circles for bubbles. Rule weights 0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.25, 2pt.
- **Shadows.** Present (notes, tabs, bubbles, photos) but **not measurable** from vector data; `--shadow-soft` is an inferred approximation.
- **Imagery.** Colour photographs with a 3pt white frame, 5pt radius, soft shadow and a numbered circular tag lower-right; comic strips in bordered panels; cut-out people on white. Never shipped, treatment only.
- **Print rules.** Folio at the bottom outer corner on every page, vertical unit label on the outer edge, section side tab alternating sides on even and odd pages.

## Iconography

No logo mark is extracted: the "ELE" wordmark on the cover and introductory pages is a stylised logotype that this system does not redraw; use the name "Agencia ELE" in type until a mark is supplied. Symbol fonts in the PDF (`ZapfDingbatsITC`, `Wingdings`, `LDecorationPi`) provide bullets and ornaments: the round and square bullets are CSS (`--bullet-example`, `--bullet-reply`); other glyphs are not shipped. The audio marker is recomposed in `AudioIcon` from its measured parts (3 x 9.5pt body, 6.2 x 8.5pt cone, arcs) rather than copied.

**Not measurable:** shadows and gradients (`unknown`; `--shadow-soft` is inferred). Radii (RAD-*) and page margins (SPC-002) are **estimated** from paths and text positions. Illustration, photo and comic colours are not tokens.

**Skipped by the scaffold:** U-001, U-002, U-004, U-006, U-007, U-008 are descriptive rows and cannot be a single token value; they are expressed as the alias tokens in `tokens/roles.css` and as guideline cards instead. The approved analysis was not edited.

**Component fidelity:** components recreate structure, colour, proportion and spacing, not glyph shapes. Handwriting faces differ visibly from the originals. The side tab text size (17pt), footer sizes and bubble text sizes were fitted from crops, not measured per element.

**Visual check:** performed (`_analysis/verify.md`): 10 component pairs, 169 of 169 tokens match. Three components (`StickyNote`, `WordBox`, `GrammarBox`) have no source cluster and were checked against page renders only, not paired. Remaining drift: `AnswerLine` (the sampled crop does not show a blank; compared with the p.10 render instead), the `SectionTab` 3 degree tilt, the lighter `Bubble` peach tint (no approved token for it), the `RunningFooter` pair (sampled crop is a p.3 text band, structure checked against p.8) and the `GrammarTable` pair (crop is the header strip only).
